Wire it up

Add this to app/index.html's <head>:

<link rel="icon" type="image/svg+xml" href="./favicon.svg">
  • The favicon itself is app/favicon.svg — click it to see the raw file, and look at this page's browser tab.
  • Black on light tab bars, white on dark ones via prefers-color-scheme so it never disappears. Delete that one @media line to keep it black everywhere.
  • The note is sized by its minimum enclosing circle and sits entirely inside the ring's inner circle, so the pictogram never collides with the ring and needs no knockouts.
  • Safari ignores SVG favicons — say the word and I'll add a PNG/ICO fallback.
