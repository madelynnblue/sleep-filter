Remove music from episodes

Finds music that plays on its own — themes, credits, interludes, songs — and cuts it out. Music under dialogue is left in place by design. Nothing is uploaded: decoding and analysis both run in this tab.

Drop episode files here or

MP4/M4A decode in-browser via WebCodecs. Other containers need a fallback backend.

    Recurring assets found

    Assets are found by looking for audio that repeats across episodes, then measured precisely. “Confidence” is chroma similarity inside the detected region — below about 0.9 means the match is weak.

    kindepisodeslengthmean startconfidence

    Segmentation is assistive, not automatic: it runs close to its decision boundary, so some episodes yield fewer segments than they should. Review the per-episode counts below before trusting the output.
